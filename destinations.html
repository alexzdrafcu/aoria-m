Our destinations

Where will your next journey take you?

From remote landscapes and vibrant cities to quiet islands far from the everyday, discover destinations chosen for their beauty, character and sense of possibility. Wherever you are drawn, we will create a journey that feels entirely your own.

Scandinavia

Where wild landscapes meet quiet sophistication

There is something wonderfully calming about Scandinavia. The air feels clearer, the light softer and the landscapes almost too beautiful to be real. Between remote fjords, colourful harbour towns and effortlessly elegant cities, every journey here feels both restorative and full of discovery.

Discover Scandinavia

South East Asia

A world that awakens every sense

South East Asia stays with you in fragments: the scent of lemongrass in a market, temple bells at dawn, lanterns glowing beside a river and the warmth of the sea at the end of the day. Every place has its own rhythm, yet the region shares an energy that makes you want to see, taste and experience more.

Discover South East Asia

New Zealand

Nature, on an extraordinary scale

New Zealand has a way of making you feel small in the best possible sense. Mountains rise straight from the water, glaciers stretch towards rainforest and empty roads lead to places that seem untouched. It is a country made for looking up, breathing deeply and staying a little longer than planned.

Discover New Zealand

African Wildlife

See the natural world at its most extraordinary

There are moments in the wild that cannot be rehearsed: an elephant appearing silently at the water’s edge, a lion calling across the plains or the first glimpse of a gorilla through the forest. You wait, you watch, and for a little while, the rest of the world falls away.

Discover African Wildlife
