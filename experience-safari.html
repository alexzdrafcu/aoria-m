Safari

Closer to the wild

A safari has a way of bringing everything into focus. The landscape, the silence and the anticipation of what may appear next make every moment feel more vivid.

From the open plains of the Serengeti to the waterways of the Okavango Delta, we create private journeys that bring you closer to the natural world without losing sight of comfort, privacy or thoughtful service.

The Experience

No two days in the wild are ever the same.

You might leave camp before sunrise, following fresh tracks through the bush, or spend a slow afternoon watching elephants gather at the water. Evenings begin with sundowners beneath an open sky and end around the fire, listening to the sounds of the landscape after dark.

We choose every lodge and camp carefully, looking beyond beautiful rooms to exceptional guiding, meaningful conservation and a genuine sense of place.

Beyond the Game Drive

Safari can be as active or unhurried as you wish.

Explore the landscape on foot with an expert guide, travel by mokoro through quiet wetlands or take to the air for a view that reveals the true scale of the wilderness. For a deeper wildlife experience, combine the savannah with gorilla trekking, chimpanzee tracking or time along the African coast.
