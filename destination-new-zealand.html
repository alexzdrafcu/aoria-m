New Zealand

Where wild landscapes meet quiet sophistication

A World Within Two Islands

New Zealand gathers an extraordinary range of landscapes into one remarkably beautiful country.

There are fjords, forests, volcanic valleys, alpine lakes and vineyards that seem to roll towards the sea. The scenery changes constantly, yet travelling here never feels hurried. The roads themselves become part of the experience, winding through open country with another remarkable view around almost every bend.

Luxury here is closely tied to the land. It is found in remote lodges, warm hospitality, exceptional local produce and the ability to reach places that feel entirely removed from the rest of the world.

The North Island

The North Island brings together subtropical coastline, Māori culture, volcanic landscapes and some of the country’s most exciting food and wine.

Sail through the Bay of Islands, explore secluded beaches and spend the afternoon swimming alongside dolphins from a private yacht. Further south, discover geothermal valleys, pristine lakes and vineyards known for their relaxed atmosphere and quietly excellent wines.

Auckland and Wellington add a more urban rhythm, with creative restaurants, independent galleries and beautiful harbour settings.

The South Island

The South Island is wilder and more dramatic.

Snow-covered peaks meet deep blue lakes, glaciers descend through mountain valleys and the fjords of the southwest feel almost prehistoric. Travel by helicopter, private vehicle or luxury rail, stopping at remote lodges where the landscape takes centre stage.

Queenstown offers adventure, fine dining and access to some of the country’s most striking scenery. Further north, Marlborough and Central Otago bring a slower pace, with vineyard lunches, long afternoons and some of New Zealand’s finest wines.

Signature Experiences

01

Sail the Bay of Islands

Spend the day aboard a private yacht, cruising past quiet coves and forested headlands. Swim, explore remote beaches and relax with cocktails and canapés as dolphins move through the water nearby.

02

Land on a Glacier

Fly by helicopter across the Southern Alps and touch down on a remote glacier. Continue to a secluded mountaintop picnic where champagne, lunch and one of the most memorable golf shots of your life await.

03

Explore Milford Sound From the Air

Take in the full scale of Fiordland from above before descending into the sound for a private cruise beneath towering cliffs and waterfalls.

04

Stay on a Working Farm

Experience New Zealand’s renowned hospitality at an intimate countryside lodge, with private walks, garden-to-table dining and a genuine connection to the land.

05

Taste Central Otago

Visit a carefully chosen selection of vineyards with a local wine expert, meeting the people behind the bottles and enjoying lunch overlooking the vines.

When to Travel

New Zealand’s summer, from December to February, brings warm weather and long days. It is ideal for the coast, hiking and outdoor dining.

Spring and autumn are quieter and especially beautiful, with mild temperatures and changing colours. Winter is perfect for alpine scenery, skiing and stays in cosy mountain lodges.

Each season offers something different, and the route can be designed accordingly.

Your New Zealand Journey

A journey through New Zealand should leave room for spontaneity.

We may plan the helicopter flight, reserve the lodge and arrange the guide, but there should also be time to stop at an empty beach, stay longer over lunch or take the road that looks too beautiful to pass by.

Whether you travel from north to south or focus on one island in depth, we will create a route that allows the country to unfold naturally.
